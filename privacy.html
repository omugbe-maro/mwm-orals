Privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026. Applies to the MWM Orals website and the MWM Orals Android app.

What we collect

Nothing. MWM Orals has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

Your study progress

Topics you mark as studied, quiz answers, encounter results and mock oral scores are saved only on your own device, in the browser or the app. They never leave it. Clearing the website's data in your browser, using the Reset button on the About page, or uninstalling the app deletes them.

Hosting

The website, and the study files the app downloads, are hosted on GitHub Pages. Fonts are served from the same site, not from a third party. As with any website, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address to deliver and secure the service; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use that information.

Links to other services

Links to YouTube, WhatsApp or email open those services, which have their own privacy policies. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send a message.

Children

MWM Orals is a study aid for adult seafarers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Contact details are on the About page.
