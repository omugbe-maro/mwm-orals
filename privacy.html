Privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026. Applies to the MWM Orals website and the MWM Orals Android app, run by Maritime With Maro.

In short

  • No advertising, and we never sell your information.
  • The website counts visits anonymously, without cookies, so we know how many people use it. You can switch this off.
  • An account is optional. Without one, your study progress stays on your device.
  • The Android app does not use visitor statistics.

Using MWM Orals without an account

You can use everything without an account. Topics you mark as studied, quiz answers, encounter results and mock oral scores are then saved only on your own device and are not sent to us.

Visitor statistics (website only)

We use Umami, a privacy-focused analytics service, to count visitors and see which parts of the website are used. It does not use cookies and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or account. When you use the website it records:

  • the pages you view and the website or link that brought you here;
  • your browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language and country (worked out from your connection; Umami does not give us your IP address);
  • anonymous counts of actions, such as starting or finishing a mock oral, solving an encounter, opening a picture, or tapping a coaching or "report a mistake" link. For a finished mock oral we record only the score band (for example 60 to 79%), never your answers.

Visits are grouped with an anonymous session identifier rather than a cookie, so we cannot tell who you are or follow you to other websites. Even when you are signed in, these statistics are not linked to your account. Umami Cloud stores the statistics on servers in the European Union and the United States and keeps them for a limited period set by our plan. We use them only to count visitors and improve MWM Orals.

Your choice: untick "Include my visits in the anonymous statistics" on the About page and your visits on that device are not counted. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, we do not count your visits at all.

If you create an account

An account is optional. It keeps your progress in step across your phone, laptop and the app. When you create one we store:

  • your email address, and the name you give us (or, if you sign in with Google, the name and email address on your Google account);
  • your role, if you choose to give it (for example cadet or officer of the watch);
  • your study progress: topics studied, quick-check and encounter results, and mock oral scores;
  • when you created the account, and when you last signed in and used MWM Orals;
  • your plan (free, or premium if paid features are offered later).

Your password is stored by our authentication provider in a scrambled (hashed) form. We cannot see it. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name and email address from Google; we get no access to your Gmail, contacts or files.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and sync your progress between devices.
  • To send account emails you ask for: confirming your email address and resetting your password.
  • To understand how many people have accounts, how often they use MWM Orals, and which roles they hold, so we can improve it.
  • To provide premium features, if we offer them and you choose to buy them.

We do not sell your information, show you ads or share it with advertisers. We do not send marketing emails.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, in its London (United Kingdom) data centre. Account emails are sent by Supabase or by an email delivery service acting for us, which receives only your email address and the message. Visitor statistics are handled by Umami Cloud as described above. The website and the app's study files are hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log technical information such as IP addresses (see the GitHub General Privacy Statement). Fonts are served from our own site.

How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it. When you delete it, your account, details, plan and synced progress are removed from our database straight away. Copies may remain in our providers' routine backups for a short period before they are overwritten.

Your rights

You can see and change your name and role on your Account page, and delete your account at any time. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it. These rights apply under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, the UK GDPR and similar laws.

Deleting your account

Sign in, open Account and choose Delete my account. If you cannot sign in, see how to delete your account.

Children

MWM Orals is a study aid for adult seafarers and is not directed at children under 16. Please do not create an account if you are under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be shown in the app or on the website.
