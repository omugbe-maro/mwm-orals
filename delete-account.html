Delete your MWM Orals account

MWM Orals, by Maritime With Maro, lets you delete your account and its data at any time, without the app.

Delete it yourself (immediate)

  1. Open the MWM Orals website and sign in with the same Google account or email address you used to sign up.
  2. On the Account page, choose Delete my account, then confirm.

Or ask us to delete it

If you cannot sign in, send a request from the email address on the account to maritimewithmaro@gmail.com, with the subject "Delete my MWM Orals account". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account, email address, name, role, plan and the study progress synced to your account. Progress saved in a browser on your own device is not affected; clear it with the Reset button on the About page.

What is kept

Nothing is kept in our database. Copies may remain in our providers' routine backups for a short period before they are overwritten.
